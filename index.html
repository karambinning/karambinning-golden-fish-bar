01 The shop

A proper chippy on Centre Point.

We've been frying fish and cutting chips for the people of King's Lynn — no fuss, no shortcuts, no microwaves.

Golden Fish Bar sits at 1 Centre Point, a short walk from the bus station and the market square. We're a takeaway shop, plain and simple — you ring ahead, we cook it fresh, you collect it hot.

What you get is what a chip shop is meant to be: fish fried to order in a clean batter, chips cut from whole potatoes, salt and vinegar on the side. That's the whole idea.

If you've not been before, ring the shop and we'll talk you through what's on today.

02 What we serve

The short list, done well.

A traditional chippy menu — fish, chips, the classic sides. We don't publish a full price list online because prices move with the market; ring the shop and we'll quote you on what you fancy.

No. 01

Fish & chips

The headline act. White fish in a crisp batter, hand-cut chips, fried fresh when you order. Salt and vinegar on request.

No. 02

Chips by themselves

Large or regular. Cooked the way they should be — golden outside, fluffy in the middle. Ask for scraps.

No. 03

Battered classics

Sausage, pie, the usual suspects in batter. Old-school chippy favourites that haven't gone out of fashion.

No. 04

Sides & sauces

Mushy peas, curry sauce, gravy, pickled bits. Ring ahead to check what's on the counter today.

No. 05

Family orders

Phoning for a few people? Tell us how many, give us fifteen minutes, we'll have it ready hot for collection.

No. 06

Not on the list?

If you don't see it here, just ask when you call. We'd rather tell you what's on than pretend to serve something we don't.
